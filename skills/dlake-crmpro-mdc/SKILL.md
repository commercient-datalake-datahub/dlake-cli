This is a capability summary, not an operating guide. To receive the operational skill you must be a
registered, whitelisted Commercient customer. Whitelisted customers fetch it with `dlake admin get_skill dlake-crmpro-mdc`

# CRMPro for your ERP and MDC

Your sales team works in MDC. Your finance and operations teams work in your ERP. CRMPro keeps the two in
step, so the people talking to customers see the same customers, contacts, products, orders and invoices that the back office is working
from, without exports, spreadsheets or rekeying.

## What it does for the business

- **Sales sees the whole customer.** Account history, open orders, invoices and what was bought, in MDC,
  where the conversation happens.
- **One version of the truth.** Customer and product records come from the system that owns them, so the two
  teams stop arguing about which spreadsheet is right.
- **Current, not end of month.** After the first load, changes keep flowing, so MDC reflects today's
  ERP, not the last export.

## What you get with the Commercient Data Lake

- A governed copy of your ERP business data, held for you, with access controlled per user and per application.
- Ready for more than CRM: reporting tools, AI assistants and your own applications read the same data.
- Standard ways in: the web app, the `dlake` command line, and AI agents connected through MCP.
- Operated by Commercient: monitoring, updates and support are part of the service.

## Who it is for

Companies running an ERP who sell through MDC and want sales, service and finance working from the same
customer, product and order information. Typical users: sales operations, finance, customer service and IT
teams responsible for the ERP.
