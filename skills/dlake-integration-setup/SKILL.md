# Integration Setup for your ERP and your CRM

Your sales team works in your CRM. Your finance and operations teams work in your ERP. Integration Setup keeps the two in
step, so the people talking to customers see the same business data that the back office is working
from, without exports, spreadsheets or rekeying.

## What it does for the business

- **Sales sees the whole customer.** Account history, open orders, invoices and what was bought, in your CRM,
  where the conversation happens.
- **One version of the truth.** Customer and product records come from the system that owns them, so the two
  teams stop arguing about which spreadsheet is right.
- **Current, not end of month.** After the first load, changes keep flowing, so your CRM reflects today's
  ERP, not the last export.

## What you get with the Commercient Data Lake

- A governed copy of your ERP business data, held for you, with access controlled per user and per application.
- Ready for more than CRM: reporting tools, AI assistants and your own applications read the same data.
- Standard ways in: the web app, the `dlake` command line, and AI agents connected through MCP.
- Operated by Commercient: monitoring, updates and support are part of the service.

## Who it is for

Companies running an ERP who sell through your CRM and want sales, service and finance working from the same
customer, product and order information. Typical users: sales operations, finance, customer service and IT
teams responsible for the ERP.

## Platforms

Your ERP, in the cloud and on-premises editions Commercient supports; your CRM.

## How to get started

- **Already a customer:** the operational skill tells you how to connect, choose what to sync and run it.
  Fetch it with `dlake admin get_skill dlake-integration-setup`, or ask support@commercient.com.
- **New to Commercient:** contact sales@commercient.com. Becoming a customer includes registration and
  whitelisting, after which the operational skills and tools are available to you.
