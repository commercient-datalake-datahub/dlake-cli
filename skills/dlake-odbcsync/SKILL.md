# ODBC Sync for your ERP

ODBC Sync keeps your customers, products, orders and invoices from your ERP available in one governed place, up to date, without manual
exports or spreadsheets.

## What it does for the business

- **One governed copy.** Your customers, products, orders and invoices from your ERP in one place, with access controlled per user and
  per application.
- **No manual exports.** The data arrives and stays current without spreadsheets or scheduled downloads.
- **Current, not end of month.** After the first load, changes keep flowing, so what you read reflects
  today's ERP, not the last export.

## What you get with the Commercient Data Lake

- A governed copy of your ERP business data, held for you, with access controlled per user and per application.
- Ready for reporting tools, AI assistants and your own applications, which all read the same data.
- Standard ways in: the web app, the `dlake` command line, and AI agents connected through MCP.
- Operated by Commercient: monitoring, updates and support are part of the service.

## Who it is for

Companies running an ERP who want their customers, products, orders and invoices in one place, current and ready for reporting, AI
assistants and their own applications. Typical users: finance, operations and IT teams responsible for the ERP.

## Platforms

Your ERP, in the cloud and on-premises editions Commercient supports.

## How to get started

- **Already a customer:** the operational skill tells you how to connect, choose what to sync and run it.
  Fetch it with `dlake admin get_skill dlake-odbcsync`, or ask support@commercient.com.
- **New to Commercient:** contact sales@commercient.com. Becoming a customer includes registration and
